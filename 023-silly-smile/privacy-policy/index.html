Welcome to Silly Smile Wallpaper! We created this app to make your phone a little more fun with cheerful, silly, and smile-worthy wallpapers.

We respect your privacy and want to keep things simple. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you use the app and how that information is used.

What Information We Collect

Silly Smile Wallpaper is primarily a wallpaper application. Most of the app's basic features can be used without creating an account or submitting personal information directly to us.

😊 In short:
We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not require your name, phone number, or personal profile information to browse and use the wallpaper features.

Depending on the features you use and the services integrated into the app, certain technical or usage information may be processed automatically by third-party service providers.

This may include:

  • App interaction and usage information
  • Crash and diagnostic information

This information may be used to maintain stability, diagnose crashes, improve performance, and understand general app usage.

Wallpaper & Photos

Silly Smile Wallpaper may need access to device storage or photos when you choose features that involve selecting, saving, or applying wallpaper images.

Images selected or saved through the app may remain on your device according to the permissions and storage behavior of your Android device.
