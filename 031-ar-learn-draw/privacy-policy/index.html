🔒 Privacy Policy · Updated September 16, 2026

Welcome to Easy Draw: Learn to Draw! We created this app to help children discover the joy of drawing through simple step-by-step lessons, cute coloring pages, and creative drawing activities.

We respect your privacy and want to keep things simple. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you use the app and how that information is used.

What Information We Collect

Easy Draw: Learn to Draw is primarily a drawing and coloring application. Most of the app's core features can be used without creating an account or submitting personal information directly to us.

🎨 In short:
We do not ask users to create an account, and we do not require a child's name, phone number, or personal profile information to use the drawing, coloring, and creative features.

Depending on the features you use and the services integrated into the app, certain technical or usage information may be processed automatically by third-party service providers.

Device & Usage Information

To help the app work properly and to understand how people interact with it, certain technical information may be processed.

This may include:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system and app version
  • Approximate technical information about your device
  • IP address or similar network information
  • App interaction and usage information
  • Crash and diagnostic information

This information may be used to maintain stability, diagnose crashes, improve performance, and understand general app usage.

Drawings, Coloring Pages & Artwork

Easy Draw allows users to create drawings, color artwork, add text or images, and save creative work.

🖌️ Your creativity stays yours.
Artwork created in the app is used to provide the drawing and editing features you choose to use. We do not use children's artwork to build a personal profile or sell their creations.

Saved artwork may remain on the device according to the app's storage behavior and the permissions or storage mechanisms available on the device.

Photos & Images

Easy Draw may need access to photos or device media when you choose features that involve adding an image to artwork or otherwise selecting media from the device.

Access to photos or media is used only when required for an action that you initiate. The app does not require access to your personal photos for its basic drawing and coloring features.

Information processed through the app and its supporting services may be used to:

  • Display and measure advertisements;

  • Detect crashes and technical problems;

Children's Privacy

Easy Draw: Learn to Draw is designed to provide a simple, child-friendly drawing and coloring experience. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information such as their name, phone number, or personal profile information in order to use the core features of the app.

If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected through our Services in circumstances where such collection is not permitted, we will take reasonable steps to address and delete it where applicable.
