ThemeMix: Wallpaper, Widget

Privacy Policy · Effective Date: September 7, 2026

ThemeMix: Wallpaper, Widget ("ThemeMix", "we", "our", or "us") is a phone customization application that helps users personalize their devices with wallpapers, themes, custom app icons, widgets, and a customizable Control Center.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use ThemeMix. We aim to collect and use information only as needed to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the features you choose to use.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, ThemeMix may process certain information from your device.

🖼️ Photos & Media Images selected by you for wallpapers, customization, or other requested features.
📱 Device Information Basic technical information needed for compatibility, diagnostics, and app functionality.
⚙️ App Usage Information Information about interactions with features may be processed to improve the application.
🛡️ Accessibility Service Accessibility access may be used for Control Center and requested system-control features.

2. Photos and Media Access

ThemeMix may request access to photos, images, or media stored on your device when a feature requires it.

Images that you select are used to provide the feature you explicitly request, such as selecting a wallpaper, customizing your home or lock screen, or using an image in a supported customization workflow.

ThemeMix does not need access to every photo on your device simply for you to browse or use the application's general customization features. Where the operating system provides a more limited photo-selection mechanism, ThemeMix may use the access granted through that mechanism.

✨ Images selected by you are used for the feature you choose to use. ThemeMix does not sell your personal photos to third parties.

3. Wallpapers, Themes, Icons, and Widgets

ThemeMix provides wallpaper, theme, icon, and widget content for personalizing your device. When you browse or save content, the application may process information necessary to display, download, save, or apply that content.

Favorite items or customization preferences may be stored locally on your device or through services required to provide the relevant feature, depending on the implementation of the application.

4. Accessibility Service

ThemeMix may use the Android Accessibility Service API to provide specific user-requested features, including the customizable Control Center and system controls.

Accessibility Service access may be used to:

  • Open the Control Center by swiping from the status bar
  • Control media playback
  • Increase or decrease device volume
  • Dismiss system dialogs when required by app features

Accessibility access is not used to read, collect, store, or share personal or sensitive user information unrelated to the requested functionality.

🛡️ ThemeMix uses Accessibility Service access only to enable the user-requested customization and system-control features described above.

5. Log Data and Diagnostics

If an error or crash occurs, ThemeMix or its integrated technical services may process diagnostic information to identify and resolve technical problems.

Depending on the services integrated into the application, diagnostic information may include device model, operating system version, application version, timestamps, crash information, app configuration, and other technical statistics.

6. Analytics

ThemeMix may use analytics technologies to understand how users interact with the application and to improve performance, stability, and features.

Analytics information may include feature usage events, session information, application version, device information, and general technical statistics.

7. Advertising

If advertising is enabled in ThemeMix, third-party advertising providers may process certain information to display and measure advertisements.

Depending on the advertising provider and your device or account settings, this may include advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, app usage information, and other information permitted by applicable privacy controls.

You can manage certain advertising and privacy preferences through your Android device and relevant account settings.

8. Third-Party Services

ThemeMix may rely on third-party providers to support application functionality, hosting, content delivery, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, security, or other technical services.

Such providers may process limited information necessary to perform their assigned services. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Examples of third-party services that may be used by ThemeMix include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Analytics services
  • Crash reporting services
  • Advertising services
  • Cloud or content delivery services

The specific third-party services used may vary depending on the application version and enabled features.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ThemeMix does not directly use browser cookies as part of its core Android application functionality.

However, third-party SDKs or services integrated into the application may use device identifiers, SDK technologies, cookies, or similar technologies where applicable for analytics, advertising, security, crash reporting, or service delivery.

10. Data Sharing

ThemeMix does not sell your personal information or personal photos.

Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to provide application functionality, maintain the service, analyze performance, display advertisements where applicable, prevent abuse, or comply with legal obligations.

11. Data Retention

Information handled by ThemeMix is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Wallpapers, favorites, preferences, and other content stored locally on your device remain under your control and may be removed by you through the application or your device, where applicable.
